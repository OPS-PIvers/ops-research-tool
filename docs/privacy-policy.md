# OPS Research Tool — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 4, 2026

OPS Research Tool ("the extension") is an internal educational tool operated by
**Orono Public Schools (Independent School District 278)** for use by its
students and staff. It helps students highlight and categorize sources while
researching, capture citations, and assemble an annotated bibliography in Google
Docs. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how it is used.

## Who this is for

The extension is provided to Orono Public Schools students and staff and is used
under the school district's authority as part of classroom instruction. It is not
offered to the general public.

## What data we collect

The extension handles only the data needed to provide its functionality:

- **Account identifier (email address).** When you use the extension, your Google
  account email (from your district Google Workspace account) is used to associate
  your work with you. Only the `userinfo.email` and `openid` scopes are requested.
- **Research content you create.** The text you choose to highlight, the category
  you assign, any notes you add, the citation fields for a source, and your
  annotations. This is content you deliberately save while researching.
- **Project selection and sync state.** Your currently selected project and a local
  queue of not‑yet‑saved items, stored on your device so your work is not lost if
  you go offline.

The extension does **not** collect browsing history, location, financial or health
information, personal communications, keystrokes, or analytics about your activity.

## How we use the data

The data is used solely to operate the tool: to save and organize your highlights
and sources, generate citations and bibliographies, and let your teachers view
class progress for assigned projects. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or
any purpose unrelated to the tool's single purpose.

## Where the data is stored

All saved research data is stored in **Google Sheets within Orono Public Schools'
existing Google Workspace for Education**, under the district's account. There is no
third‑party server, database, or analytics service — data does not leave the
district's Google Workspace environment, which is governed by Google's Workspace for
Education agreement and the district's data‑privacy practices.

## Who can see the data

Your saved research is visible to **you** and to **your teachers and school
administrators** within the district, for the projects you are assigned to. Data is
not sold, rented, or shared with any third party, and is never used to determine
creditworthiness or for lending.

## Browser permissions

The extension requests only the permissions required for its function:

- **storage / unlimitedStorage** — keep your project selection and an offline queue
  of pending work on your device until it syncs.
- **alarms** — periodically retry syncing your saved work after a brief disconnect.
- **identity** — obtain your Google identity token (email/openid only) so your work
  saves to your own account.
- **scripting / host access (`<all_urls>`)** — show the highlight toolbar and
  research panel on the pages you research.
- **script.google.com / script.googleusercontent.com** — send your saved highlights
  and citations to the district's Google Apps Script web app, the only backend.

All executable code is packaged in the extension; no remote code is downloaded or
run. The extension only exchanges data with the district's own Apps Script service.

## Data retention and deletion

Research data persists in the district's Google Workspace for the duration of the
project and is archived by school year per district practice. To request correction
or deletion of a student's data, contact the address below; the district will act on
the request in accordance with applicable education‑privacy law.

## Children's privacy

The extension is used by students under the direction of Orono Public Schools as an
educational tool. The district acts as the school official with a legitimate
educational interest, consistent with FERPA and the district's obligations under
applicable student‑privacy laws.
